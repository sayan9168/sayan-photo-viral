Who is Sayan Mahata?

Sayan Mahata (also known as Sayan The Researcher and sayan9168) is a System Architect, Security Researcher and technology founder from Medinipur, West Bengal, India.

He is the Founder of Sayanox Private Limited and the creator of the original Sayanox programming language (file extension .sa). His work spans compilers, systems programming, cybersecurity research, OSINT tools and open-source developer tooling.

About Sayanox and key work

Sayanox is an original systems programming language created by Sayan Mahata. Key characteristics include self-hosting capability, native AOT compilation for multiple architectures, and the distinctive .sa file extension.

Beyond the language itself, Sayan Mahata develops and maintains a range of research and production tools under the Sayanox ecosystem and independent open-source projects focused on security, developer experience and systems software.
